# Roadmap

## Next product integrations

- **Voice:** Spanish spoken explanations and audio replies; explicit consent, accessible controls, provider capability checks and real delivery tests.
- **Rich iMessage cards:** native actionable summary/task cards on supported dedicated Photon lines; verified payload/claim identity behavior and plain-text fallback.
- Reaction-based claims only after target-message/actor semantics are verified on the real serving line.

## Before production

- Individual account authentication and verified family enrollment; multiple families, backup helpers and fine-grained permissions.
- Distributed inbox ownership and a verified provider idempotency contract for outgoing sends.
- Encrypted durable storage, retention/erasure policies, backups, incident review and provider retention disclosure.
- Shared rate limiting, stronger operational monitoring and explicit failed-delivery disposition.
- Family-local deadline/reminder scheduling, multiple-document support and HEIC conversion.
- Human review/correction of extraction, additional OCR checks and independently measured accuracy on representative consented/synthetic mail.

No voice, rich-card, reaction-claim or production-readiness success is claimed by this implementation.
